We welcome proposals for **papers** and **posters** on any topic related to vocabulary research. Details are below.

{{< card_text "Topics" >}}

You are welcome to submit proposals on the following vocabulary-related topics.

{{< cfp_item "Lexical representation" >}}
How words are stored and organized in the mind — their forms, meanings, and links to other words — in first, second, heritage, and multilingual speakers.
{{< /cfp_item >}}

{{< cfp_item "Lexical processing" >}}
How words are recognized, retrieved, understood, and produced in speech and writing, including how related meanings and concepts are activated.
{{< /cfp_item >}}

{{< cfp_item "Vocabulary learning and teaching" >}}
How learners pick up and internalize new words, and teaching practices that help L1 and L2 users build their word knowledge.
{{< /cfp_item >}}

{{< cfp_item "Assessment" >}}
Measuring vocabulary knowledge and development: breadth (how many words are known), depth (how well they are known), and fluency (how readily they can be used).
{{< /cfp_item >}}

{{< cfp_item "Corpora and technology" >}}
Corpus-based vocabulary research, word lists, and digital tools for learning, teaching, and researching vocabulary.
{{< /cfp_item >}}

{{< cfp_item "Research methods" >}}
Approaches, procedures, and tools for collecting and analyzing data on vocabulary acquisition, use, teaching, and assessment.
{{< /cfp_item >}}

{{< /card_text >}}

{{< card_text "Submission Guidelines" >}}

{{< cfp_item "Presentation formats" >}}
**Papers** — 30-minute slots: 20 minutes for the presentation and 10 minutes for discussion.

**Posters** — interactive sessions, well suited to presenting information visually and to in-depth discussion with other researchers.
{{< /cfp_item >}}

{{< cfp_item "Number of submissions" >}}
Each person may submit only **one** proposal as a single or first author, whether a paper or a poster. No one's name should appear on more than **three** proposals in total.
{{< /cfp_item >}}

{{< cfp_item "Language" >}}
The conference language is English, so proposals and presentations should be primarily in English. If you study a language other than English, examples and study materials may be in that language.
{{< /cfp_item >}}

{{< cfp_item "Anonymity" >}}
All proposals receive double-blind peer review. Please remove anything that identifies you or your co-authors from the title, abstract, and references — for example, cite your own earlier work as "(Author, 2024)".
{{< /cfp_item >}}

{{< cfp_item "Abstract format" >}}
**Title:** maximum 15 words (capitalize only the first word and proper nouns).

**Abstract:** maximum 300 words, including in-text citations.

**References:** entered in a separate box, and only for works cited in the abstract. They do not count toward the 300 words.
{{< /cfp_item >}}

{{< /card_text >}}

{{< card_text "How to Submit" >}}

1. **Prepare your proposal first.** Write your title, abstract, and any references in a text editor so you can check the word counts and keep a copy.
2. **Open the submission site** at [submit.vocab-at-hiroshima.org](https://submit.vocab-at-hiroshima.org/).
3. **Enter the author details** — name, affiliation, and email for each author, with the presenting author marked.
4. **Choose your format** (paper or poster) and the topic that best fits your proposal.
5. **Paste in your title, abstract, and references**, then submit before **23:59 JST on February 15th, 2027**. Please do not leave it until the last hour.

If you need to change something after submitting, please contact us — do not submit a second copy.

{{< /card_text >}}

{{< card_text "Evaluation Criteria" >}}

Each proposal is reviewed anonymously by multiple reviewers, who assess it on:

- Relevance and significance of the topic
- Theoretical framework and connections to existing research
- Research design / conceptual framework
- Organization and clarity

{{< /card_text >}}

{{< card_text "Registration Policy for Accepted Proposals" >}}

If your proposal is accepted, you must register for the conference by **May 15th, 2027** to keep your place on the program. For proposals with more than one author, at least one author must register and present. If you can no longer attend, please let the committee know as soon as possible so the slot can be offered to someone else. By confirming, you agree to present at the date and time assigned by the program committee; we are not able to take requests for specific time slots.

{{< /card_text >}}

{{< card_text "Questions" >}}

For questions about the submission process or other technical matters, please contact [info@vocab-at-hiroshima.org](mailto:info@vocab-at-hiroshima.org).
