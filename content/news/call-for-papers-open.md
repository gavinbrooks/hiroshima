The call for papers for **Vocab@Hiroshima 2027** is now open.

We welcome proposals on all aspects of vocabulary learning, teaching, and assessment. Full details, including presentation formats and the submission link, are on the [Call for Papers](/call-for-papers/) page.

**Abstracts are due by February 15, 2027.**

The conference will be held at the International Conference Center Hiroshima from September 15 to 17, 2027, with the summer school on September 13 and 14.

If you have any questions about submitting, please contact us at [info@vocab-at-hiroshima.org](mailto:info@vocab-at-hiroshima.org).

We look forward to reading your proposals.
